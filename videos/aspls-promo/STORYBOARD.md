## Video direction

- **Palette:** from `frame.md` (code-editorial remix) — warm-cream / GitHub-ink canvas roles; accent `#0969DA` as scarce voltage; mono surfaces for code; never pure black void or purple glow.
- **Motion grammar:** VO-paced reveals (`power3` long-tail); one signature move per frame; hold still after resolve; subtle jitter only on holds. No front-load-then-freeze; no screensaver drift.
- **Rhythm:** Frames 1–2 kinetic typography; Frames 3–4 UI proof (held screenshots); Frame 5 list assemble; Frame 6 calm CTA hold.
- **Never:** fake download stats, browser chrome chrome-over-chrome, stock “AI purple”, MusicGen BGM, inventing assets not in `asset_candidates`.
- **Caption keep-out:** top ~83% for primary content.
